## Tools used, not distributed

[Blender](https://www.blender.org/) 4.1 (GPL; run as a command-line tool, its output is data),
Chromium, ImageMagick, and - for the KTX2 experiment only (`tools/ktx_layers.py`) - the Khronos
[KTX-Software](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/KTX-Software) `ktx` encoder (Apache-2.0).

Lettering on signs is built from fonts installed on the building machine (found with `fc-match`);
no font files are in the repository, and built models carry letter outlines as geometry.

## Methods

The vertex-light refit follows L. Kavan, A. W. Bargteil and P.-P. Sloan, "Least Squares Vertex
Baking", EGSR 2011 (<https://users.cs.utah.edu/~ladislav/kavan11least/kavan11least.pdf>).
`MSFT_lod` is the glTF vendor extension specified at
<https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/tree/main/extensions/2.0/Vendor/MSFT_lod>.

## Art direction

Every model, material and texture is generated by the code in this repository; no third-party
meshes, textures or scans are included. The layout and mood were developed against private
reference boards in the spirit of lantern-lit storybook town squares; those images are not part of
the repository.
