The baked town from its packed format and from the glTF files of tools/bake2glb.mjs, one variant per page load. One load is one measurement: open with the browser cache off (DevTools) for a cold one. The numbers appear below and in window.__bench.

The live bake no longer keeps its packed files (the .glb files are what the town loads): "packed" below is now the page's own loader reading those .glb files. The other variants read files exported from an earlier bake.
